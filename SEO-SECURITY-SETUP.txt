VOLTIX MOBILE — SEO, SPEED & SECURITY NOTES

Prepared while the site is running locally in VS Code Live Server. Search engines cannot crawl localhost.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Choose your hosting provider and publish the site over HTTPS.
2. Rename sitemap.xml to sitemap.xml.
3. Replace every YOUR-DOMAIN.com in sitemap.xml with your real domain.
4. In robots.txt, remove the # from the Sitemap line and replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com.
5. Upload robots.txt and sitemap.xml to the website root (the same folder as index.html).
6. Submit https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.

WHAT WAS CHANGED
- robots.txt allows public pages and disallows cart, checkout and the demo orders page.
- sitemap.xml lists only the public pages: home, about, products and contact.
- Cart, checkout and orders pages are marked noindex/nofollow.
- The large PNG logo was converted to WebP and HTML references updated.
- Product/content images now load lazily and decode asynchronously.
- JPEG images were recompressed while keeping their filenames.
- _headers contains security headers for Netlify hosting. Other hosts need equivalent headers in their own configuration.

SECURITY LIMITATIONS
This is a front-end demonstration. localStorage orders are stored in the visitor's browser and are not a secure, shared order database. The orders.html page is not protected by a login. Do not use it for real customer data or real payments until a backend, authentication, server-side validation and a secure payment provider are added.

The contact form and checkout also need a secure backend/service to actually deliver messages or process orders. Client-side validation alone is not security.
